# Password reset and account unlock

This is a fictional support article for a demonstration system.

If you remember your current password, open the fictional Employee Portal, select Profile, and choose Change Password. If you have forgotten it, choose Forgot Password at the sign in page, confirm your demo email, and follow the one time verification instructions.

If the account is locked, wait 15 minutes and retry once. If you still cannot sign in, request an Account Unlock incident. Never share your password, verification code or recovery details with an agent.

The demo process does not reset a real account or send email.
