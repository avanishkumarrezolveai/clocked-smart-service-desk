# Device loss and replacement

If your company owned laptop or phone is lost, inform the service desk and security team promptly. The demo will record the user's description without attempting device tracking or remote wiping.

If a device is damaged or unusable, the user can request a replacement or loaner through the corresponding service offering. If a previously working device failed, an incident may also be needed. Confirm what the user wants before opening multiple items.

When more than one registered device matches, ask for manufacturer and device type before requesting serial number or hostname.
